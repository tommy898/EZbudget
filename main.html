How to use this App

  • This Home page shows how much money you spent in total this month.
  • Go to "Add" to add a spending to start tracking your money.
  • You can set your monthly budget in "Manage"
  • See settings for language, dark/light mode

Privacy Notes

  • localStorage is used to store your data.
  • Specifically, it stores all your spending in a list.
  • It also stores your monthly budget, which is a single number
  • Lastly, it stores your theme preference (default/dark)

The font and icons are Google Font All other content made by Tommy Z.
